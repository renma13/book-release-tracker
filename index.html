Goodreads shelf

Go to your To-Read shelf on Goodreads, scroll to the bottom of the page, and copy the link behind the small orange RSS icon. Paste the full URL below — it works even if your profile is private, and needs no password or login.

By default, syncing tries a few public proxies in turn — free, but occasionally slow, rate-limited, or offline. For reliable syncing, deploy the tiny free proxy in proxy-worker.js (included in this project) to your own Cloudflare Worker (a few clicks, no cost) and paste its URL here.

Release dates

Goodreads often only stores a book's original publish year, not a future edition's date, so release dates come from Hardcover, a book-tracking site that keeps accurate upcoming release dates. Create a free account, open your account settings → Hardcover API, click New API Key, and paste it below.
